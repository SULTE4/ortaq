# Ortaq — the lights we leave on

## Subject and argument

The audience is the university defense jury and Kazakhstan's apartment residents. The one action is to watch the process, then explore the same building's real updates.

Specific material: Astana winter; Kabanbay Batyr 60; 120 apartments; 3 entrances; 9 floors; apartment 45's cold radiator; a lit kitchen window; a shared courtyard; the chairman's responsibility; 18 monthly receipts; advisory apartment votes; the word Ortaq and the shared home it represents.

Claims and proof:

1. Every window is someone's home → the actual 120-window, API-driven building.
2. A problem belongs to people, not a ticket queue → apartment 45's heating story, neighbours, plumber and photographed resolution.
3. Care becomes visible → live status pins and readable monthly spending.
4. The same place connects all three apps → the window motif, warm light and editorial type shared by the resident, management and 3D experience.

## Direction

Use the dealt **one-motif** direction: the apartment window becomes the organizing shape, a luminous architectural object in 3D, a cropped illustration on the resident welcome, and the active navigation/metric treatment in management. Preserve deep blue and white; take the warm accent from winter window light. No ornamental eyebrow labels or generic three-card feature row.

Idea sentence: On this page, **120 Astana apartment windows** become **one living portrait of a shared home**.

The live thing: select a real issue / apartment, see its actual place in the building, and watch apartment 45's scripted journey without changing live records.

## Audit of the baseline

The old screenshot is a pale miniature model surrounded by equally weighted floating boxes. Tiny tracked labels, a generic sans face, a cool blue wash, and a flat slab building make the place feel administrative. The login uses fake flat towers rather than the actual model. Every data category competes for attention. The resident hero duplicates an illustration above its real 3D card; admin uses three identical metric cards. The cinematic gives a feature tour, without first making the resident matter.

Rework all three surfaces. Prioritize the 3D show: dusk, warm windows, architectural material, human scale details, an uncluttered stage and intentional camera. Keep the functional controls and privacy rules. Use the subject's own code-generated world rather than unrelated stock photos. The four-image marketing-page floor does not apply to this interactive application; its own actual 3D scene is the dominant media. Existing app controls keep their interface conventions.

Typography redraw: the first dealt Switzer font permits self-hosting but restricts redistribution of font files in repositories. This deliverable must remain reproducible and shareable, so the type slot was redrawn once: **Libre Franklin / Source Serif 4**, both bundled under SIL OFL from Google Fonts. The serif voice carries the human story; the gothic face carries controls and figures. Seed: 2003435766. No stock images stand in for this building.
